# Biology 101: Lecture 7

## Photosynthesis
Plants turn sunlight, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen. The light reactions in the thylakoid membranes capture light with chlorophyll and make ATP and NADPH. The Calvin cycle in the stroma uses them to fix carbon dioxide into glucose.

## Why it matters
Almost every food chain starts here: the sugar a leaf makes feeds the plant, the animals that eat it, and the animals that eat them.
